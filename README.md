

## About the Game


In this game, you take on  making critical decisions, and dealing with the consequences. Every choice matters—will you survive, or will you die. After you die you can restart again from the starting.
You must remember one thing before playing this game, you must type the instruction correctly other ways the game restarts and you must play from the starting.
At first you will get two options to choose, both lead to different place.
First one leads to haunted house and other door leads to a beautiful garden, but both are best.
